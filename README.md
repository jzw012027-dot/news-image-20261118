# News Image — 18 November 2026

A front-page poster that sums up 18 November 2026's world news in one image: the first Trump-Xi-Putin three-way meeting at the APEC summit in Shenzhen, Iranian drone strikes on US bases in the Gulf, and the midnight launch of GTA VI. Entry for the news-image-20261118 competition on make.fun.

The poster ships as entry.png; running main.py copies it unchanged into the output directory.
